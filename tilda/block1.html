Koh Lanta travel guide

Tours & excursions from Koh Lanta

Island hopping & snorkeling

Day trips from Koh Lanta to Phi Phi Islands (Maya Bay, Pileh Lagoon, Bamboo Island), Koh Rok & Koh Haa — the best snorkeling near Lanta — and the 4 Islands tour with the Emerald Cave on Koh Mook, Koh Kradan and Koh Ngai, by speedboat or traditional longtail boat.

Mangroves, kayaking & sightseeing

Kayak into the Talabeng sea cave, explore the Tung Yee Peng mangrove forest by kayak or longtail, visit Lanta Old Town, the viewpoint, the sea gypsy village and the lighthouse in Mu Ko Lanta National Park.

Jungle, elephants & activities

4x4 jungle safari, an ethical no-riding elephant sanctuary, Thai cooking classes, fishing at Koh Maa, zipline and ATV adventures — with hotel pick-up from anywhere on Koh Lanta.

Motorbike & car rental

Rent a scooter, motorbike or car on Koh Lanta and get it delivered to your hotel or villa. Choose the exact bike, the start date and the number of days online.
